# Northwind Robotics GmbH: Leave Policy (version 3, valid from 1 January 2026)

Northwind Robotics is a fictional company. This policy exists only to test the LeaveDesk assistant.

## P1 Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive
a share in proportion to their contracted working days (for example, 3 days a week gives 14.4 days, rounded up to
15). New employees accrue 2 days for each full month of employment in their first year.

## P2 Carry-over

Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be taken by
31 March; days not taken by then expire. More than 5 days can only be carried over with written approval from HR,
for example after long sick leave.

## P3 Requesting annual leave

Requests are made in LeaveDesk. A request may cover at most 20 working days. Requests of more than 3 working days
must be submitted at least 2 weeks before the first day of leave. A request may not overlap another pending or
approved request of the same employee.

## P4 Approval

The employee's manager approves or rejects a request within 5 working days. If the manager has not decided after
5 working days, the request is escalated to HR automatically. A manager cannot approve their own request; those
requests go to the manager's own manager.

## P5 Cancelling leave

A pending request can be cancelled by the employee in LeaveDesk at any time. An approved request can only be
cancelled by the manager, on the employee's request. Cancelled days are returned to the employee's balance.

## P6 Sick leave

Employees who are sick must tell their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. From the 4th calendar
day of sickness, a doctor's certificate is required. Sick days do not reduce the annual leave balance. Sick leave
is recorded in LeaveDesk as a SICK request.

## P7 Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to 20 working days per calendar year can be requested with a reason. It needs manager approval
and must be requested at least 4 weeks in advance. Unpaid leave does not reduce the annual leave balance.

## P8 Public holidays

Public holidays of the employee's work location do not count as leave days. LeaveDesk counts Monday to Friday
only; holidays must be checked with the local HR team until holiday calendars are added to LeaveDesk.

## P9 Leave not covered by this policy

Parental leave, special leave (for example for a wedding or a death in the family), sabbaticals and leave under
statutory protection are handled by HR directly and are not part of LeaveDesk.

## P10 Contact

Questions this policy does not answer go to HR: hr@northwind.example.
